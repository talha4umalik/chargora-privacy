Privacy Policy

Chargora (package com.talhaworkspace.batterychargealert)
Last updated: 4 October 2026

Summary

Chargora does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app works entirely on your device.

Information we do not collect

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and does not request internet access. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores only your settings (which alerts are enabled, snooze duration, sound and vibration choices) and the state of the current charging session on your device, so alerts work correctly after a restart. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications – to show the battery alarm and the monitoring status.
  • Foreground service – to keep watching the battery level while the app is closed.
  • Run at startup – to resume monitoring after the phone restarts.
  • Full-screen notification – to show the alarm screen over the lock screen.
  • Vibrate and wake lock – to vibrate and keep the alarm sounding while the screen is off.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

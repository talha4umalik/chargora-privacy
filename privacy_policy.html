Privacy Policy

Chargora (package com.talhaworkspace.batterychargealert)
Last updated: 7 October 2026

Summary

Chargora works on your device. We do not run any server, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect or sell your personal data ourselves. The app is free and shows one advertisement through Google AdMob, which is the only reason the app uses the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings (which alerts are enabled, alert level, snooze duration, sound, vibration and voice choices), the state of the current charging session, and your charging history (start and end time, battery level, duration and battery temperature of each charge) on your device, so alerts work correctly after a restart and so you can see your history. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can delete the history at any time in the app.

Advertising

Chargora shows a single banner advertisement at the bottom of the Charging History screen. It is clearly labelled "Advertisement". Advertisements are never shown on the alarm screens, in Settings, or next to alarm, snooze or stop buttons.

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure advertisements, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) and information about how you interact with advertisements. Chargora does not give Google any battery, charging-history or settings data. Please read Google's information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change this choice later in the app's privacy options when offered, or by clearing the app data.
  • Advertisements are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Information we do not collect

Chargora does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information, and it has no account system or analytics of its own.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications – to show the battery alarm and the monitoring status.
  • Foreground service – to keep watching the battery level while the app is closed.
  • Run at startup – to resume monitoring after the phone restarts.
  • Full-screen notification – to show the alarm screen over the lock screen.
  • Vibrate and wake lock – to vibrate and keep the alarm sounding while the screen is off.
  • Internet and network state – only to load the advertisement.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob for advertising, as described above.

Children

Chargora is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
